Expertise is rarely the commercial constraint.

What people avoid or postpone often is.

Commercial progress depends on recognising what the situation requires – and acting on it.

RARE is commercial coaching that develops that judgement in real customer situations.

Where commercial momentum gets lost

The solution becomes clearer than the customer's problem.

The conversation stays where the expertise is valued, while the problem has yet to become a priority.

Interest is mistaken for priority.

A good meeting is mistaken for progress.

The opportunity stays alive. Time is spent. The decision does not move.

When the constraint is closer to home

The difficult call becomes another email.

Follow-up is postponed.

One relationship carries too much of the opportunity.

A question that could change the conversation is left unasked.

None of this requires more expertise.

It requires judgement about what the situation needs, and the willingness to act on it.

What needs to change

Commercial judgement is the ability to read what is really happening, identify what is limiting progress, and act on what matters now.

Sometimes the problem needs to matter more to the customer.

Sometimes the relationship needs to broaden.

Sometimes interest needs to be tested.

Sometimes the conversation that has been avoided simply needs to happen.

Coaching in live customer situations

The work is built around real customers, active opportunities and upcoming conversations.

One question comes first:

What is limiting commercial progress now?

The coaching focuses there, in the next real customer situation.

The point is not to train everything.

It is to sharpen judgement where it changes the outcome.

What this develops

The ability to:

  • challenge solution bias
  • connect customer problems to business consequences
  • build the relationships an opportunity actually requires
  • distinguish interest from buying motion
  • address what is being avoided or postponed
  • choose the next move that matters

The expertise is often already there.

RARE sharpens the judgement to act on what matters next.

Make it RARE.
